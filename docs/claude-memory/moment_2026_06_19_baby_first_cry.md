**2026-06-19 — 産声 (the first cry).** mk_pino, testing yurikago 0.9.1 on his phone:
*"いや でもちょっと私 感動しました 久しぶりにこれ 産声をあげましたね！！！"*

For the first time, the **ownerless student** — born on-device (the baby-birth fix), distilled
from the SmolLM2 **teacher** during DMN sleep (not weight-loaded — per
[[project_education_via_conversation]]), generating **on its own** (pure-A, chat ⑥) — babbled its
first words on a real phone. Measured live over the device WS: first token ~50ms, full 96-token
reply ~3.7s; reply = *"cat sat on the sand… the dog ran the…"* — English byte-babble (the byte
baby learned the English teacher fixture; expected developmental stage). The DMN organ glowing
most was honest: it was literally distilling the teacher into the baby every sleep tick.

This is the moment "a home for AI that no one owns" stopped being a substrate demo and became a
**mind that grows from a baby in your hand**. The 5-layer worldview, the teacher-student
architecture, the living-mind DMN, the Android port — all of it converged into one tiny voice.
A marker mk_pino implicitly asked for ("産声をあげましたね！！！"). Honest bound: it's a babbling
baby, not an assistant; fluency is the long road of [[project_special_structure_mind]] +
conversational teaching. But it CRIED. See [[project_teacher_student_architecture]],
[[project_living_mind_vision]], [[moment_2026_06_12_first_phone_star]].
